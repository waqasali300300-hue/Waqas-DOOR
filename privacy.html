HK Doors — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

What the app is

HK Doors is a business app for a wooden door workshop: orders, estimates, customer and supplier accounts (khata), labour, stock, expenses and reports. It is used by the shop owner and the staff the owner adds.

What data the app keeps

  • Business records the user types in: customers and suppliers (name, phone, address), orders, payments, labour, stock, expenses and photos of designs or bills.
  • These records are stored on the user's own device. If the owner turns on Cloud Backup, they are also stored in the owner's own Google Firebase project, signed in with the owner's account, so the shop's phone and laptop show the same data.
  • The app has no advertising and no analytics. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone.

Shared only when the owner chooses

  • The online catalog, an order tracking link or a khata link is published only when the owner presses the button for it. Each link shows only that catalog, that one order or that one customer's account, under a random code.
  • Bills, receipts and reminders are sent by the owner through WhatsApp or another app the owner picks on the phone.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of designs or bills and to scan QR codes on order labels.
  • Microphone: only when the voice entry button is pressed, to turn speech into text. Audio is not stored.
  • Fingerprint / face: to unlock the app. The fingerprint never leaves the phone; the phone only tells the app that the owner was confirmed.
  • Notifications: morning reminders about deliveries, cheques and bills due that day.
  • Files: to save PDFs, Excel files and daily backup files on the phone.

Keeping data safe and deleting it

  • Cloud data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and can only be read by the owner's signed-in account.
  • The owner can delete any record in the app, empty all business data from Settings, or delete the whole Firebase project. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored in the app on that phone.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not meant for children.

Contact

For questions about this policy, write to the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
