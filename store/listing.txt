Full description (max 4000):
HK Doors is a complete business app for wooden door makers and shops.

★ Orders & estimates — size, design and quantity; rate, wood (cft), labour and profit are worked out automatically.
★ Party khata — every order and payment in one ledger, old khata entries, PDF statements and a live khata link for the customer.
★ Labour — karigar accounts, advances, weekly/monthly reports and one-tap payment.
★ Stock — wood stock, hardware, purchases, suppliers and stock counting.
★ WhatsApp — bills, receipts and payment reminders sent as a neat picture with your logo.
★ Design album & instant price — show designs to the customer and give a price on the spot.
★ Production board — cutting list, workshop board and delivery-date alerts.
★ Reports — monthly profit, design report and the full year's account.
★ Safe data — works offline, cloud sync between phone and laptop, daily backups, fingerprint and PIN lock, staff with limited access.
★ Urdu and Roman Urdu.

Darwaze banane walon aur dukanon ke liye mukammal app: orders, khata, mazdoori, lakri ka stock, WhatsApp bill aur reports — sab ek jagah.
